## 1. Executive conclusion

**CP is significantly more capable in repository source than a high-level “tenant registry/resolver” description suggests.** Current main includes canonical Organisation/Tenant and corporate relationships, admission/verification, legal-actor mandates and progressive onboarding, exact context and capability resolution, provider certification/instance/identity profiles, governed developer bootstrap, federation/Principal authority, grants and changesets, provisioning/convergence, estate client/commerce/resource provenance, Trade/ERP/Payments legal-actor decision handlers, event and observability foundations, and a Next.js confidential Console with Organisation 360 and indexed directory.

These are **implemented source/CI surfaces with different qualifications**. They are **not** 25 or 30 newly approved Shared platform capabilities. CP is **the canonical platform authority**, not an ordinary provider declaring executable capability support through an engine's `.baobab/capability-provider.yaml`. Its internal administrative/context/identity and resolution operations must not be accidentally published as provider grants. Domain engines remain owners of their business vetoes, money movement, accounting, identity proofing, trade commitments and content publication.
